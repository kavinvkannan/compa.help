01Why Compa

Official responders can't be everywhere. Neighbors already are.

In every disaster, the same truth emerges: official responders are stretched thin, and neighbors save neighbors. Compa is a mutual-aid app that makes that instinct instant, organized, and available to everyone — the community layer that emergency plans have always been missing.

Compa was born from lived experience — losing everything in the Chennai floods, then evacuating Miami ahead of Hurricane Irma with no affordable flight and gas stations running dry along the evacuation route. We built the tool we wished existed: one place where a community becomes its own safety net.

"The first people on the scene of any disaster are never the professionals. They're the people who were already there."

  • ✓Ask in plain language. No forms, no jargon — just say what you need.
  • ✓Get matched nearby with people who've offered exactly that kind of help.
  • ✓Trust local updates — shelters, closures, and safety checks from your own neighborhood.
02How it works

One network for the full cycle of disaster.

Emergencies have three acts. Most apps only show up for one. Compa stays for all three.

Act I

Prepare

Household readiness checklists, neighborhood drill coordination, and plain-language guidance — before the skies turn grey. Know your people before you need them.

Blue-sky mode
Act II

Respond

When it hits: request help or offer it in seconds, coordinate with CERT teams and volunteers, and follow verified local updates — shelters, closures, wellness checks.

Grey-sky mode
Act III

Recover

Recovery takes months; headlines last days. Compa keeps the same network together for cleanups, supply drives, rebuilding, and checking on the vulnerable long after.

04For agencies

Extend your reach into every neighborhood.

No agency can be everywhere when disaster strikes. Compa organizes the community layer — residents, volunteers, and CERT teams — in the spirit of FEMA's Whole Community doctrine.

CERT & volunteer coordination

Activate CERT members with clear tasking, and channel spontaneous volunteers into useful, supervised roles — designed to fit ICS-based volunteer staging.

Ground truth for your COP

Community-reported, locally verified insight — blocked streets, wellness checks, shelter capacity — feeding your common operating picture and the lifelines FEMA prioritizes.

Equity by design

Plain-language first, multilingual, low-bandwidth friendly — built to WCAG 2.2 AA so the most vulnerable residents aren't left out of the response.

Complementary, never competing

Compa doesn't replace 911, public alerting, or NIMS/ICS command. It's the community layer alongside them — with CAP-compatible, open-standards design.

06Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

Is Compa free?

Yes — free for residents. An optional Pro tier and agency partnerships will sustain it, but the core mutual-aid network stays free for the people who need it.

When does it launch?

We're in private development, piloting with select communities before public launch. Join the waitlist — you'll be first to know, and first in.

Does Compa replace 911 or official alerts?

No. Compa complements official emergency management — the community layer alongside 911, public alerting, and agency response. Always call 911 in an emergency.

Is Compa affiliated with FEMA?

No. Compa is an independent product of Integer LLC, inspired by FEMA's Whole Community doctrine. We are not endorsed by, affiliated with, or acting on behalf of FEMA or any government agency.
