For government & emergency management

Extend your reach into every neighborhood.

No agency can be everywhere when disaster strikes. Compa organizes the community layer — residents, volunteers, and CERT teams — so your preparedness, response, and recovery efforts reach further, in the spirit of FEMA's Whole Community approach.

Apply for the pilot

01Capabilities

The community layer your plans are missing.

Emergency operations plans cover agencies and assets. Compa covers the people — organized, reachable, and ready to help each other.

Neighborhood-level engagement

Preparedness campaigns that actually land. Reach residents where they live with household-level readiness guidance, drill coordination, and plain-language alerts — before, during, and after events.

Volunteer & CERT coordination

Give CERT teams and spontaneous volunteers one organized place to deploy. Post needs, assign offers, and track who's doing what — without the chaos of group chats and spreadsheets. Designed to fit ICS-based volunteer staging.

Ground-truth community insight

See what official sensors can't: blocked streets, neighbors needing wellness checks, open shelters with capacity. Community-reported and locally verified — ground truth that feeds your common operating picture and strengthens the lifelines FEMA prioritizes: food, water, sheltering, and communications.

Equity & accessibility by design

Disasters hit hardest where access is thinnest. Compa is built plain-language first, multilingual, and low-bandwidth friendly — so vulnerable residents aren't left out of the response.

02For CERT programs

A force multiplier for your CERT team.

CERT volunteers are trained to help — Compa gives them somewhere organized to do it, and gives you a way to direct the spontaneous volunteers who show up anyway.

  • ✓Activate and deploy your CERT members with clear tasking, in plain language.
  • ✓Channel spontaneous volunteers — unaffiliated neighbors who arrive ready to help — into useful, supervised roles instead of freelancing.
  • ✓Wellness checks at scale: coordinate door-knocks for older adults, people with disabilities, and others with access and functional needs.
  • ✓After the response: keep the same network for recovery — cleanups, supply drives, and rebuilding, months after the headlines fade.
03Our commitment

What Compa is — and isn't.

Trust is everything in emergency management. So let's be precise.

Compa is

  • ✓A complement to official emergency management and public alerting
  • ✓A free tool for residents, volunteers, and community organizations
  • ✓Built for the full cycle: prepare, respond, recover

Compa isn't

  • ✕A replacement for 911 or official emergency alerts
  • ✕A substitute for NIMS/ICS command structures or agency authority
  • ✕Endorsed by or affiliated with FEMA or any government agency

Our commitments to public-sector partners

  • ✓Accessibility: building to WCAG 2.2 AA and plain-language standards from day one.
  • ✓Security: NIST-aligned security practices on our roadmap; happy to discuss your requirements during the pilot.
  • ✓Interoperability: open-standards design (Common Alerting Protocol–compatible) so Compa fits your existing systems.
  • ✓Your data stays yours: community data is never sold, and agencies control what they share.

Compa is built by Integer LLC, a Florida AI solutions provider with delivered client work across South Florida.

04Questions

Common questions from agencies.

What does a pilot cost our agency?

Pilot partnerships are structured collaboratively — our goal is learning, not revenue. We'll discuss a scope that works for both sides before anything begins. Compa is designed to support grant-eligible activities under programs such as EMPG, SHSP, and UASI — confirm eligibility with your State Administrative Agency or FEMA regional office.

How does Compa handle data privacy and security?

Privacy is foundational. Residents control what's shared and with whom, location is used only to connect people with nearby help, and we never sell personal data. Our public privacy policy and security practices will be published before launch, and we're happy to discuss your requirements during the pilot.

Is Compa accessible to residents with disabilities?

Accessibility is a design requirement, not an afterthought. We're building to WCAG 2.2 AA and plain-language standards, with low-bandwidth and multilingual support — because the people most at risk in disasters are often the least served by technology.

Does Compa replace our existing alerting or EOC systems?

No. Compa complements official systems — it organizes the community layer that sits alongside 911, public alerting, and NIMS/ICS structures. It doesn't replace agency authority or command.

Is Compa affiliated with FEMA?

No. Compa is an independent product of Integer LLC. We're inspired by FEMA's Whole Community doctrine, but we are not endorsed by, affiliated with, or acting on behalf of FEMA or any government agency.
